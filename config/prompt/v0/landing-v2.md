# Format: landing page

You arrange the page from fixed components and write their prose. Choose the arrangement that suits this customer, this occasion and these picks. Do not fall back on one shape. Code renders each component.

- occasion_key: the key given in the task, exactly.
- sections: 3 to 6 components, in reading order.

| type | what you give | limits |
|---|---|---|
| hero | slot, heading, line | Shows one pick large. heading: 2 to 8 words, no token, belongs to this pick. line: 8 to 25 words, and only this pick's token |
| story | text | 15 to 50 words. Any pick's token |
| product_grid | items, each with slot and caption | 2 to 4 cards. caption: 4 to 18 words, no token, belongs to its own pick |
| pairing | slots (2), line | Two picks that belong together, by use or on one table or outfit. line: 8 to 30 words, and only these two picks' tokens |
| quote | quote_id | A line from Nicobar's own pages. You write nothing |

Layout rules:

1. The first section is a hero, and there is only one.
2. Every pick appears in exactly one hero, product_grid or pairing. Story and quote show no pick. Divide the picks first: with 4 picks, the hero takes one and a grid takes 3. With 5, the hero takes one and the other 4 go in a grid of 4 or two groups of 2. With 6, the hero takes one and the other 5 go in a group of 3 and a group of 2. A group of 2 is a grid or a pairing.
3. At most 2 stories and at most 1 quote. Never place a story and a quote, or two stories, next to each other.

Quotes are optional. E4 "Light the diya, set a flower down, and begin." is for the festive occasion only. E6 "For your table, or someone else's" is for birthday, housewarming and hosting only. On any other occasion, use no quote.

Each token appears at most once across the whole page. Reader-visible prose is every heading, line, story and caption. Rendered titles and the quote do not count, but a token in your prose does.
